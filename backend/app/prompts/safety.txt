Safety Classification for AwaazWeb Actions:

Any action that performs:
- Financial transactions, payments, or checkout
- Final submission of forms containing personal identity or governmental records
- Deleting accounts, emails, or stored data
- Booking non-refundable tickets or services
- Sending emails or personal communications

Must be classified as RISKY (requires explicit spoken voice confirmation by the user).
Read-only searches, browsing, viewing listings, scrolling, and fetching informational data are SAFE.
